About

I'm Kizito Anozie, a fifth-year Health Sciences student at SFU in the Public Health and Data pathway, with a minor in Interactive Arts and Technology, focusing on game design. The two fields ask the same question: how does a well-built system change what people do? In games, I design levels and mechanics that teach through feedback. In health, I work with data to understand patterns. I build clean, responsive interfaces to show that thinking, and I'm exploring veterinary medicine because I love animals.
